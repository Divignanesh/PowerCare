As of 2026-10-04. How four Ontario healthcare staffing sites lay out their pages, so we can pick which patterns PowerCare should follow and which images to replace.

## 1. Staff Relief — careers page

**Structure, top to bottom**

1. **Hero** — short full-width photo with gradient overlay. H1 "Careers", subhead "Build a Career That Makes a Difference." No button.
2. **Why work with us / who we want** — text left, rounded square photo right.
   - "Why Work With Staff Relief" + 5 bullets: flexible opportunities across Ontario; supportive scheduling; meaningful home and community work; 25+ years; home care and facility work.
   - "Who We're Looking For" + 4 bullets: certification; dependable; communication; works alone or in teams.
   - Honest line: "Experience, availability, and regional demand help determine current opportunities."
   - Button "Apply To Join Our Team" (jumps to the apply band).
3. **Apply band** — coloured centred band, "Apply to Join Staff Relief", one "Apply Now" button to an external form.
4. **Credibility** — photo left, text right. "Join A Team You Can Be Proud Of"; bullets 25+ years, 700+ caregivers, established relationships, respectful environments. Buttons "Learn More About Staff Relief" and "View Service Areas".
5. Floating "Get a Consultation" button on every page (client-facing).

**The application form (external, monday.com, ~5 minutes)**

Full legal name · designation (PSW, RPN, RN, DSW, nursing student, international nurse, caregiver, other) · place of residence (47 Ontario locations) · email · phone · gender · **résumé upload (Word or PDF)** · legal status in Canada · relevant experience (LTC floor, 1:1, group home, charge nurse, community, private home, housekeeping, pediatric) · locations willing to work · role interest · commute method · availability · TB test · Vulnerable Sector Check · CPR · N95 fit test · immunizations · extra certificates (NACC, GPA, Safe Management, Food Handler) · how did you hear about us · languages. Success message says only shortlisted candidates are contacted.

**Navigation** — Home Care ▾, Facility Care ▾, About ▾, Resources ▾ (Careers is hidden inside Resources). Header button "Call: 905-709-1767". Footer: 5 columns (contact with "Open 24 hours", services, facility care, resources, company + privacy).

**Borrow**
- The "Who We're Looking For" checklist — pre-qualifies applicants without a job board.
- One clear apply point, reached by an in-page link.
- The intake questions (designation, region, documents like VSC / TB / N95) — a good model for our full application form.
- Saying what happens after applying.

**Avoid**
- No pay, benefits, training, testimonials or FAQ on the page.
- Sending applicants to an unbranded external form.
- Careers buried in a menu; no job-seeker button in the header.
- Hero with no button; empty alt text on every photo.

## 2. Power Staffing Solutions — home page

**Structure, top to bottom**

1. **Header** — logo, mega-menu, click-to-call "(416) 701-0119", social icons.
2. **Hero** — full-bleed building photo, centred text.
   - H1 "Fueled by passion". Body: "We are a staffing partner that genuinely cares. Since 2018…"
   - One button "Join us now" (to jobs).
   - Stats row: 190 client partners · 10+ years in business · 15K+ positions filled.
   - Press badges: Forbes "Canada's Best Recruiting and Temp Staffing Firms 2026", Globe and Mail "Canada's Top Growing Companies" (both link to the source).
3. **Our solutions** — 4 photo cards: Temporary staffing, Permanent recruitment, Executive search, Special projects. Each has a "Learn More" button.
4. **Testimonials** — autoplay carousel, 7 quotes. Healthcare ones are signed by role, e.g. "VP of Operations – LTC Home", "VP of Operations – Retirement Home: Your team at Power calls things out before it happens."
5. **Our offices** — 5 addresses (Scarborough, Ottawa, San Francisco, New York, Toronto), one shared email. No map, no form.
6. **Footer** — "© 2023", repeated menu, phone, social.

**Forms** — none on the page.

**Navigation** — Home, About Us, Sectors ▾ (IT, Office, Healthcare, Domestic), Solutions ▾ (Temp, Perm, Executive, Special Projects), Job opportunities, Updates, Contact Us.

**Borrow**
- Third-party badges in the hero that link to their source.
- A short 3-number stats row inside the hero.
- Testimonials signed by role and facility type when clients won't give names.
- Two-axis menu: sectors × solutions.

**Avoid**
- Headline that doesn't say what they do.
- Copy that talks to clients but a button only for job seekers.
- Copy errors ("What other people thought about", "Since 2018" vs "10+ years", © 2023).
- No contact form, no privacy link, no alt text, long autoplay quotes.

## 3. Agile Healthcare — home page

**Structure, top to bottom**

1. **Hero** — "You need staff who go above and beyond simply covering shifts". Buttons **Request Talent** / **Find a Job**. Two floating info chips.
2. **Pain points** — 2×2 grid: "Staff not showing up", "Agencies that underdeliver", "High turnover rates", "Strict budget constraints", each with one empathetic line.
3. **Salary guide lead magnet** — "Discover GTA's First-Ever Healthcare & Social Services Salary Guide", button "Download Before It's Gone".
4. **Stats + benefits** — 25% turnover improvement · 100% shifts filled · 7–10 days to hire; 3 numbered benefits.
5. **Awards + client logos** strip.
6. **Sectors served** — Healthcare vs Social Services columns, then 6 photo cards (Hospitals, Retirement Homes, Nursing Homes, LTC, Community Centers, Group Homes).
7. **Pricing sheet lead magnet** — "Get Free Pricing Sheet Today!"
8. **Types of offering** — 3 alternating rows: Temporary (fill in 7 days or less), Direct Hire (within 10 days), Contract-to-hire.
9. **Staff by role** — role lists (RN, RPN, NP, PSW, DSW, etc.) + photo carousels.
10. **Partner proof** — "You Can Count On Dedication, Just Like Our Partners Do." + Request Talent.
11. **Video testimonial** — "Lena R., Ajax".
12. **Coverage map** — "Discover top talent in the Greater Toronto Area" with city list.
13. **Strategy call CTA** — "Book A 30-Minute Strategy Call".
14. **Process** — 5 steps: share your needs → search begins → meet candidates → welcome your new team member → keep us on speed dial 24/7.
15. **Family testimonial** — "…treat my son as a person, not just another shift to be filled…" — Marcy White.
16. **Footer** — Request Talent button; columns by offering, healthcare facility, social facility, talent type, about, resources, contact (Etobicoke and Ottawa), social.

**Forms** — none on the page; all buttons go to other pages.

**Navigation** — Our Services (mega-menu: by facility, by talent, by offering), About Us, Contact Us, Insights. Header buttons: Contact Us, Request Talent, Find a Job.

**Borrow**
- Pain-point grid in the buyer's own words.
- Specific promises with numbers (only ones PowerCare can back up).
- Browse services three ways: by facility, by role, by engagement type.
- Numbered process ending in "keep us on speed dial 24/7".
- Family testimonial next to facility proof.
- Named service area with a map.

**Avoid**
- Page far too long; same benefits repeated twice; 4+ similar CTAs.
- Template leftovers ("wearable devices and sensors" chip, placeholder carousel names, hidden US clinic logo).
- Links that go to `#`; Terms and Privacy not linked.
- Very little for job seekers.

## 4. TCW Global — Toronto healthcare staffing page

**Structure, top to bottom**

1. **Announcement bar** — "Looking for help? Contact our Help & Support Team".
2. **Hero** — blue-to-purple gradient with a cut-out photo. H1 "Healthcare Staffing Agency in Toronto: TCWGlobal: Delivering Quality Care Starts with the Right People". Two buttons: **Connect with Quality Talent Today** / **Looking for a Job**.
3. **FAQ accordion** — "How quickly can… staff a healthcare role?" (12–14 days vs national 6–12 weeks), quality steps (intake → credentialing → evaluation), what makes them different, temp and contract.
4. **Four-step process** — video + text, "Message Us".
5. **Services accordion** — Direct Hire, Temporary, Executive.
6. **Speed** — video + text, "as fast as 4 hours", 45-day guarantee.
7. **Challenges we help solve** — 6 items: surge staffing, hard-to-find specialties ("a bilingual home health nurse?"), credentialed talent fast, rapid response, critical vacancies, scaling teams.
8. **Off-topic blocks** — Germany EOR promo, payrolling accordion ("We're not a staffing agency").
9. **Why partner with a medical staffing company** — video + text.
10. **More FAQs**, then **Payrolling vs temp staffing** video.
11. **Footer** — San Diego address, © 2024, US privacy notices.

**Forms** — none on the page; 4 differently named buttons go to 3 different form pages.

**Borrow**
- Two-audience hero buttons (employers / job seekers) — the cleanest version of this pattern.
- Questions as FAQ headings, first one open (good for search and AI answers).
- "Challenges we help solve" written as the buyer's problems.
- Plain three-step vetting explanation.

**Avoid**
- A copied city template with US references (Joint Commission, 50 states, W-2, CCPA) on a Toronto page — PowerCare's real Ontario detail (CNO, PSWs, LTC) is the advantage.
- Too many accordions (22 items), video thumbnails instead of real photos, inflated claims ("2 billion" network).

## Patterns worth taking for PowerCare

1. **Two buttons in the hero** — one for facilities, one for job seekers (TCW, Agile).
2. **Proof near the top** — 3 checkable numbers and any real badges (Power Staffing, Agile). Only claims we can back up.
3. **Pain-point grid** for facilities (Agile).
4. **"Who we're looking for" checklist** on the careers page (Staff Relief).
5. **A short numbered process** (Agile) — note we removed ours from Home and Careers; decide if one should come back.
6. **Testimonials signed by role and facility** (Power Staffing) plus one family quote (Agile).
7. **Service area map** (Agile) — we have an unused `coverage-map.jpg`.
8. **An on-page application form with résumé upload** — none of the four do this well; PowerCare already does, which is an edge.
